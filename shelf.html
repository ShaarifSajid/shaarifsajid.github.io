Books

Favorites from 2026

  • Odyssey Stephen Fry Reading this made me dive deep into Greek myth, and I ended up reading all of Fry's other works in this series as well.
  • Food Sense Christopher Gardner Dr. Gardner is a scientist-philosopher who really makes you rethink plant-based eating and see through a lot of diet fads.
  • A World Appears Michael Pollan A thorough study of consciousness, which feels especially relevant now that we're getting close to AGI.
  • Flesh David Szalay A pretty good read, but mostly part of my effort to just read more fiction.
