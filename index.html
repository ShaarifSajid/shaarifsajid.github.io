03 Projects

Google Agents for Impact Hackathon · Atlanta · Oct 2026

Public Health Equity Mobile Care Broker

A multi-agent system that decides where a county's mobile health clinic should park each Saturday, and explains the choice in plain language to the people who live there. Built in one day on public federal health data for Fulton County, GA.

How it works

  • Three agents, shared state. A planner, an equity auditor, and a communicator, built with Google ADK and Gemini on Vertex AI, hand off work through session state.
  • Race is an audit, never an input. The planner ranks census tracts on clinical need, uninsurance, and transit access. The auditor sees demographic data the planner is denied, rejects the first-pass plan if it skews, and sends it back for a second pass.
  • Grounded in real data. Disease burden, shortage areas, and safety-net sites live in BigQuery, queried through Google's managed BigQuery MCP server. Deployed on Cloud Run.

My role

  • Data engineering: built the shortage-area tract table and siting criteria the planner reasons over.
  • Agent orchestration: wired the two-pass plan-and-audit loop between planner and auditor, plus the tools and tests behind it.
  • UI: simplified the front end for non-technical users and fixed the 4-week schedule view.

FOCI '18 · USENIX Workshop, co-located with USENIX Security

A Bestiary of Blocking: The Motivations and Modes behind Website Unavailability

M. C. Tschantz, S. Afroz, S. Sajid, S. A. Qazi, M. Javed, V. Paxson

With ICSI (UC Berkeley) and LUMS. We measured server-side blocking, where a site turns visitors away by location rather than a government or ISP blocking it: geoblocking EU visitors to avoid GDPR, country-based blocking, and security-driven blocking. I ran multi-region crawls and traceroute analysis to tell server-side blocking apart from network interference.
