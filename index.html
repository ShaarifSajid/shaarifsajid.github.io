01 About

I'm a software engineer at Corpay, where I build high-reliability API integrations for a high-volume airline crew logistics platform used by hundreds of users across global airline partners. My background spans full-stack development (C#/.NET, React, and TypeScript) as well as cloud security research from my time at Georgia Tech, where I studied Internet-scale threat infrastructure. Outside of engineering, I'm into coffee (enough that I built a pour-over timer app just to dial in recipes), soccer and tennis, and reading, mostly non-fiction on physics and philosophy.
